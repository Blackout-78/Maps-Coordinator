Maps Coordinator v0.2.2: zoom all the way out, faster, crash report.

Added:
- Zoom all the way out to see the whole state: major roads and city names. The first time you zoom out, it prepares this view from your imported map (one time, can take a minute). No re-import needed.
- Last crash report in Settings, with a Copy button, so a crash can be diagnosed without internet.

Improved:
- Smoother panning and zooming: street names and detail pause while your finger is moving, and roads are drawn in batches.
- Less memory use; the app recovers instead of crashing when memory is tight.
- The screen no longer restarts when you rotate the phone.
- Importing a file with points after roads now shows a clear message instead of failing.
